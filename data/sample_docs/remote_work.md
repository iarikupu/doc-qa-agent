# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a director and are reviewed every six months.

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, which are 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone. Meetings are scheduled inside core hours whenever possible.

The company provides a one-time home office stipend of 500 dollars for new remote employees. Internet costs are reimbursed up to 40 dollars per month.
