# Expense Reimbursement

Employees submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase. Reports submitted later than 30 days are rejected unless a manager approves an exception.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Alcohol is never reimbursable. Flights must be booked in economy class for trips shorter than six hours.

Reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle after approval. Receipts are required for any single expense above 25 dollars.
