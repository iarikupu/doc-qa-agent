# Onboarding Guide

New engineers receive their laptop and accounts on their first day. The first week focuses on setting up the development environment and shadowing a teammate on a small ticket.

Every new hire is assigned a buddy who answers questions during the first three months. A 30-60-90 day plan is agreed with the manager in the first week.

Engineers ship their first change to production within their first two weeks. Code reviews require at least one approval from a teammate before merging.
